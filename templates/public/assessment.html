Security assessment

This page describes how the portal is tested. It lists what to test, not what has been found. Findings belong in your own report, supported by evidence.

In scope

  • This application at http://127.0.0.1:5000 on your own computer
  • Student and admin sign in, registration, profile and password change
  • All admin pages and every form field
  • Session cookie, response headers and error pages

Out of scope

  • Any website or server you do not own
  • Denial of service or load testing
  • Other people's computers on your network
  • The separate security lab, which has its own README and runs on port 5001

Rules of engagement

  • Test only on a copy you created, with fictional data
  • Keep Burp Suite's proxy and the portal on 127.0.0.1
  • Record evidence for every claim, and retest after each fix
  • Do not publish credentials, even test ones
